Privacy policy for Synchro Race Timer

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Synchro Race Timer (“the App”) times performance runs and synchronizes starts. We design the App to keep as much data as possible on your device. Publisher: Maxsoft Software. Contact: biuro@maxsoft.pl.

Data processed on your device

  • Location / GPS — used for speed, distance, altitude and slope checks during timing sessions. Not uploaded to Maxsoft Software servers.
  • Motion sensors — accelerometer and related sensors for launch detection and sensor fusion. Processed locally.
  • Bluetooth — used for peer-to-peer synchronized starts with nearby phones. Device names and addresses stay on participating devices for the session.
  • Run history and preferences — stored locally on your phone, including the optional Pro entitlement cache.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Synchro Pro) are handled by Google Play Billing. Purchase status is verified with Google. Maxsoft Software does not receive your full payment card details.

Advertising

If you are not a Pro user, the App may show ads via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) on non-timing screens and occasionally after a saved run. Google may process advertising identifiers under Google’s policies. Pro users do not see these ads.

Permissions

The App may request precise location, Bluetooth scan, connect and advertise, and related runtime permissions required for timing and sync.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by Google (Play Billing, AdMob, Play services location) solely to provide those features.

Retention

Local timing history remains until you clear app data or uninstall. Google may retain purchase and ad records per their policies.

Your choices

  • Deny location or Bluetooth permissions (core timing and sync features will be limited).
  • Upgrade to Synchro Pro to remove ads.
  • Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local history.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated. The current address of this page is https://www.maxsoft.pl/en/aplikacje-mobilne/synchro-race-timer/polityka-prywatnosci/. The same address is listed on the app’s Google Play page.
