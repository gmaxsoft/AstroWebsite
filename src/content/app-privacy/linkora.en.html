Privacy policy for Linkora

Effective date: 10 November 2026. Last updated: 10 November 2026.

This policy describes how the mobile game Linkora – DotPuzzle handles information. Publisher: Maxsoft. Contact: biuro@maxsoft.pl.

Summary

  • Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device.
  • The app may show advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • We do not require an account and do not ask for your name or password.
  • You can reset local progress inside the app settings.

Information we process

Information stored on your device

The app stores locally:

  • unlocked and completed levels,
  • stars and scores,
  • sound, music and vibration preferences,
  • the current level and layout seed needed to restore a board.

This data stays on your device unless you back up the device with Google or manufacturer tools.

Advertising partners (Google)

If ads are enabled, Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, device and app technical data, and interaction data (for example ad views and clicks) to show, measure and personalize ads, and to manage consent where required.

Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob and privacy.

Permissions

Internet is used to load ads and consent forms when available. The app does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

  • Legitimate interests or contract necessity — providing core gameplay and storing local progress.
  • Consent — non-essential advertising identifiers where required, managed via the Google UMP consent form.

You can change consent choices through the system or ad settings, or by reinstalling and seeing the consent form again where applicable.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own profiles. Advertising partners may still process identifiers under their own policies. Parents or guardians can write to biuro@maxsoft.pl to request deletion of any data under our control.

Data retention

  • Local progress remains until you reset it in the app or uninstall / clear app data.
  • Ad partners retain data according to their own retention policies.

International transfers

Google and other service providers may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States. Where required, those providers apply appropriate safeguards.

Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. For local game data, use Reset Progress in Settings or clear the app storage in Android settings. For advertising identifiers, reset the advertising ID in Android settings. For other requests, email biuro@maxsoft.pl.

Security

We take reasonable technical measures appropriate for a local-first mobile game. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated. The current address of this page is https://www.maxsoft.pl/en/aplikacje-mobilne/linkora/polityka-prywatnosci/. The same address is listed on the app’s Google Play page.
